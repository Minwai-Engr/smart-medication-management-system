# Smart Medication Management and Dispensing System

**University of Victoria | ENGR 110 — Engineering Design Project**

## Project Overview

This project focused on developing a conceptual design for a smart medication management and dispensing system intended to help older adults manage their medications safely and conveniently.

The goal was to propose a system that could support medication scheduling, improve accessibility, reduce medication management difficulties, and provide reminders and notifications.

The project was completed as part of a four-member engineering design team at the University of Victoria.

**Project Type:** Conceptual Engineering Design  
**Status:** Design proposal — no physical prototype developed

## Problem Statement

Medication management can be challenging for older adults, particularly when multiple medications must be taken at different times.

Our team investigated how an automated medication management system could improve convenience, accessibility, and medication organization while considering safety and affordability.

## Design Objectives

The proposed system was evaluated against several engineering requirements:

- Ease of use and accessibility
- Affordability
- Compact design
- Medication storage and preservation
- Safety and child-resistant access
- Ease of refilling and cleaning
- Accurate medication dispensing

## Engineering Design Process

### 1. Research and Requirements Analysis

We investigated medication management challenges and reviewed existing products to identify user needs and design constraints.

### 2. Concept Generation

The team developed five conceptual designs using engineering design methods, including a functional means tree and morphological chart.

The concepts explored different approaches to medication storage, dispensing, authentication, scheduling, and user notifications.

### 3. Concept Evaluation

We used weighted design criteria and a numerical evaluation matrix to compare the five proposed concepts.

The evaluation considered factors such as usability, safety, affordability, and functionality.

### 4. Selected Design

Based on the team's evaluation, **Design #2** was selected as the proposed solution.

The selected concept included:

- Stainless steel medication storage
- Modular medication compartments
- Sliding lid
- Fingerprint authentication
- Mobile application for medication scheduling
- Speaker and LED reminders
- SMS notifications for caregivers
- Electronic dispensing mechanism
- Display interface

These features formed part of the proposed design and were not implemented in a physical prototype.
